1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TrilokCloud Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("TrilokCloud", "we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you visit the AskShala marketing website at askshala.in ("Website") or submit a demo request through our contact form.

AskShala is an AI school information assistant for Indian schools. This policy applies to the public Website only. Schools that deploy AskShala receive a separate privacy notice for parents and staff who use the assistant at their school.

TrilokCloud Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is the data fiduciary for personal information collected through this Website.

3. What we collect

When you request a demo or contact us through the Website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • School name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Approximate student count range
  • How you heard about AskShala (optional)
  • Any message you choose to include (optional)

We do not require you to create an account to browse the Website. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

4. How we use your information

We use contact form information to:

  • Respond to demo requests and sales enquiries
  • Schedule conversations with school decision makers
  • Improve how we explain AskShala to prospective schools
  • Keep a record of inbound interest for follow-up

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use contact details for unrelated marketing without your consent.

5. Legal basis (India)

We process your information in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and other applicable Indian law. When you submit the contact form, you provide consent for us to use your details to respond to your enquiry. You may withdraw consent by emailing us, subject to any records we must keep by law.

6. Where data is stored

Contact form submissions are stored in secure systems operated by TrilokCloud and trusted service providers (for example, Google Workspace spreadsheets used for lead tracking). Data is accessed only by authorised team members who need it to respond to your request.

7. How long we keep data

We retain contact enquiries for as long as needed to respond, evaluate a potential school partnership, and maintain reasonable business records. If you ask us to delete your details and we have no legal reason to keep them, we will do so within a reasonable time.

8. Sharing with others

We may share your information only when necessary:

  • With TrilokCloud staff involved in sales or onboarding
  • With service providers who help us store or manage enquiries under contract
  • When required by law, court order, or government authority

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based
  • Request erasure in permitted circumstances
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in certain situations
  • File a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India if you believe your rights have been violated

To exercise these rights, email askshala@trilokcloud.in. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Grievance redressal

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal information, contact us at askshala@trilokcloud.in. We aim to acknowledge grievances within 7 business days and resolve them within 30 days where possible.
