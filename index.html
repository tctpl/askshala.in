HOW IT WORKS

Three things to know.

  1. 1

    Your Content Only

    AskShala learns from your school's own documents. Fee schedules, admission guidelines, bus routes, timetables, and policies. It answers only from these. Nothing is invented. If it does not know something it says so and gives your contact details directly.

  2. 2

    Every Answer Cites Its Source

    After every answer parents see exactly which school document the information came from. Your school stays in control of what is communicated. Fully auditable.

  3. 3

    On Your Existing Website

    No app download. No new login system. One line of code added by your web team. Works on any website platform. Live in under 5 minutes.

FIRST DEPLOYMENT

The First Deployment

A leading CBSE-affiliated senior secondary school in Bengaluru became the first institution to deploy an AI-powered parent information assistant built on AskShala. The school serves students from Nursery to Class XII and has been a part of the Bengaluru education community since 1990.

The assistant was configured with the school's own published content covering curriculum, admission process, examination rules, school timings, and contact details. It answers parent questions accurately, cites the source document with every response, and handles questions it cannot answer by directing parents to the school directly.
