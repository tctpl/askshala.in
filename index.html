HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from documents to live answers.

  1. 1

    Upload your documents

    School admins upload PDF, DOCX, and TXT files through the admin panel. Fee circulars, admission brochures, calendars, transport routes, and policy documents. No technical skills required.

  2. 2

    Your private knowledge base

    AskShala prepares your content in a private knowledge base scoped to your school only. Your documents stay isolated from every other institution on the platform.

  3. 3

    Embed on your website

    No app download. No new login system. Your web team adds one snippet and the chat widget goes live on WordPress, Wix, or custom HTML in minutes.

  4. 4

    Parents and students ask instantly

    Questions are answered in seconds from your approved documents, with the source cited on every response. If AskShala does not know the answer it says so and gives your school contact details directly.

The First Deployment

A leading CBSE-affiliated senior secondary school in Bengaluru became the first institution to deploy an AI-powered parent information assistant built on AskShala. The school serves students from Nursery to Class XII and has been a part of the Bengaluru education community since 1990.

WHAT YOU GET

Built for Indian schools from day one.

Every feature in AskShala was designed with Indian schools, Indian parents, and Indian data law in mind. Not adapted for India after the fact. Built for it from the start.

Answers Only From Your Documents

Parents ask in plain language. AskShala answers from PDF, DOCX, and TXT uploads with source citations on every response. If the information is missing it falls back to your school phone and email. It never guesses.

DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

Personal information shared in queries is handled in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Built for Indian data law from day one, not retrofitted.

Always On

Parents get answers at midnight on a Sunday. During exam season. During admission rush. During holidays. AskShala does not take leave, does not get sick, and does not have a busy signal.

Instant Responses

Complete Audit Trail

Every admin action is logged with a timestamp. Who uploaded a document. Who deleted it. When. Your school has a complete record of everything that has happened in the system.

Your Brand. Your Identity.
